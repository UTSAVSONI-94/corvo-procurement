CORVO ENGINEERING - POLICY AMENDMENT MEMORANDUM
Date: 2026-11-05
Reference: AMEND-2026-002
Effective Date: 2026-11-05

Subject: Plant-Specific Spot PO Threshold for Chakan (CHK1)

In view of expanded automotive assembly volume at the Chakan facility (CHK1):
1. Rule 5.4 (Plant CHK1): For delivery plant CHK1, the Spot PO threshold is increased to Rs 3,50,000 where recent 365-day purchase history exists.
2. Requisitions for plant PUN1 continue under standard corporate limits of Rs 2,00,000.

Approved by: Head of Procurement
