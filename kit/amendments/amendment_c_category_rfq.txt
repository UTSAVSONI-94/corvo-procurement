CORVO ENGINEERING - POLICY AMENDMENT MEMORANDUM
Date: 2026-11-12
Reference: AMEND-2026-003
Effective Date: 2026-11-15

Subject: Mandatory Competitive Quotes for Safety and PPE Category

To ensure compliance with industrial safety audits:
1. Rule 5.3b (Inserted before Rule 5.4): Any requisition under category "Safety / PPE" exceeding Rs 50,000 must be routed to competitive RFQ with minimum 3 supplier quotes, regardless of prior single-source history.
2. Effective from 2026-11-15.

Approved by: Head of Procurement
