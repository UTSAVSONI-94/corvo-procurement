CORVO ENGINEERING - POLICY AMENDMENT MEMORANDUM
Date: 2026-11-01
Reference: AMEND-2026-001
Effective Date: 2026-11-01

Subject: Adjustment to Operational Spot PO and Emergency Limits

Pursuant to the executive procurement review, the following threshold adjustments are approved:
1. Rule 5.4 (Spot PO Limit): The maximum threshold for Spot Purchase Orders without competitive quotes is raised from Rs 2,00,000 to Rs 3,00,000.
2. Rule 5.3 (Emergency Cap): The emergency purchase authorization ceiling is raised from Rs 5,00,000 to Rs 7,50,000.

All other clauses of Corvo Engineering Procurement Policy v5.0 remain in full effect.
Approved by: Head of Procurement
